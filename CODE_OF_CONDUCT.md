# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct (summary)

We pledge to make participation harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender, experience, nationality, or religion.

- Be respectful, constructive, and welcoming.
- Cricket rivalries stay friendly. No personal attacks, no hate speech.
- Violations: report to maintainers (see `SECURITY.md` for contact handling). Maintainers may warn, temp-ban, or permanently remove.

Full text: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
